# Planning folder for SDLC documents.

## SDG & Justification
We will be focusing on SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being. It aims to promote healthy lives and improve well-being for everyone. Nowadays, many individuals lack the proper tools to improve or maintain a healthy lifestyle. In addition, people around the world are very caught up in their jobs or studies, they spend most of their time to complete their daily responsibilities. This poses a serious problem when it comes to promoting healthy living. Without a proper support tool, it can lead to long term health issues.<br><br>
  


## Problem Statement
In universities, students face a multitude of challenges, whether it can be from academic pressure and stresses due to many factors, especially financial matters, assignments and examinations. If these problems left unchecked, they could ultimately lead to academic burnout. Many students will also struggle to balance their studies with personal responsibilities, social life and also self care. According to the World Health Organization (WHO), burnout is described as a state cause by prolonged stress that not handled well. <br><br>



## Product Vision Statement
For university students who struggle to maintain mental fitness, Burnout Detection Companion (BuDCo) is a software system designed to monitor their internal exhaustion on a daily basis. Unlike any other burnout tracking software, our product is intended on providing simple yet effective features along with an easy-to-use user interface. <br><br><br><br><br>
